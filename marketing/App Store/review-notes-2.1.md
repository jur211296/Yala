# Notas para el revisor — 2.1

Subidas a App Store Connect el 2026-10-21 (versión iOS 2.1, build 20). Cuenta demo: no requerida.

```
No demo account is needed: Yala works without an account, and data stays on the device and in the user's private iCloud.

Optional "Yala cloud account" (Settings › Where your data lives, or from the welcome screen): signs in with Apple or Google and syncs data to our backend.

Groups: sign in with Apple or Google, accept the consent screen, then create a group and add expenses. Members join through an invite link shared from the group; seeing a split between two people needs two Apple or Google identities.

Account deletion: Profile › Your Yala account › Delete account (visible once signed in). It deletes the data on our servers and revokes Sign in with Apple / Google.

Pro: the sandbox purchase unlocks Pro. Without Pro you get 5 voice notes and 5 photos to try the AI features.
```
