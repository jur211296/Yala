# App Store Description — English (EN)

## App Name
Yala

## Description

Tracking your expenses has never been this easy.
Yala is your smart financial ally. Snap a photo, dictate by voice, or import your files. Yala sorts it out. You get on with your day. No typing. No hassle.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH YALA:
- Smart capture: snap a photo of your receipt and Yala's AI extracts amount, date, and category automatically.
- Voice that understands: dictate expenses naturally. Say "Spent 20 dollars at the grocery store" and Yala logs it.
- Import everything: upload CSV or Excel bank statements and Yala organizes every transaction for you.
- Siri Shortcuts: automate your frequent expenses with a single tap or voice command.
- Smart budgets: set limits by category and get alerts before you overspend.
- Multi-account: track cash, cards, and bank accounts in one place.
- Clear reports: see where your money goes with charts by category, monthly trends, and small expense detection.
- AI Insights: get personalized analysis of your spending habits and tips to improve your finances.
- Scheduled payments: never miss a due date with automatic reminders.
- 45+ currencies: manage multiple currencies with live exchange rates.
- Customize everything: choose your theme, icons, and make Yala yours.

PRIVACY FIRST:
Your financial data is stored exclusively on your device. We don't connect to banks, don't sell data, and store nothing on servers. Your information is yours. Period.
